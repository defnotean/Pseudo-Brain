## Exact live features

Replay is batch size one and uses the exact live reference step. At every root,
the updater consumes the internally owned prior action. The current applied
action is recorded separately only as a factual evaluation label and is
installed as pending controller history after current inference. Actual reward,
hazard, targets, intervention assignment, environment seed, episode/root IDs,
and current factual action are forbidden as scorer inputs.

The 485-dimensional masked superset is:

- `N[0:120]`: post-update belief;
- `B[120:240]`: old belief;
- `Z[240:360]`: current RGB encoder latent;
- `E[360:480]`: live cognitive prediction error;
- `A[480:485]`: internally owned prior-action one-hot.

The old `P` flag is structurally omitted, but its source column must first be
exact positive one at every post-burn-in root. Every tensor is detached,
float32, finite, C-contiguous, and every inactive slot is canonical positive
zero. The six frozen masks are:

- N: N;
- Z: Z;
- BZ: B+Z;
- NZ: N+Z;
- NBZ: N+B+Z;
- U0: B+Z+E+A.

## Masked-superset fit and literal deployment pruning

Every arm is fitted with the identical 87,961-parameter topology:

`Linear(485,120) -> ReLU -> parameterless layer_norm(eps=1e-5)`; a
`Embedding(5,120) -> parameterless layer_norm(eps=1e-5)` action path; concatenate;
`Linear(240,120) -> ReLU -> Linear(120,1)`.

The registered recipe is
`masked_superset_train_pruned_deployment_v1`. It intentionally preserves
fan-in-485 initialization and optimizer dynamics during fitting. It does not
claim equivalence to training a compact head from initialization.

The grid is three FIT cohorts by initialization seeds `{41042,42042,43042}`.
Within each cell all six arms begin byte-identically. All heads share the same
32 full relative root permutations from seed 50042. Each pass has 3,072 roots,
batch size 24, exactly 128 optimizer steps, and each head has 4,096 steps. The
total is 54 heads and 221,184 steps. Optimization is fresh AdamW, learning rate
`1e-3`, weight decay `1e-4`, clip norm 1, unweighted all-action BCE. There is no
early stopping, adaptive schedule, retry, or DEV-based choice.

After all 54 fits and before any CAL/DEV source construction, the first linear
layer is pruned by exact active columns while its bias and every downstream
tensor are copied byte-exactly. Literal counts are N/Z 44,161; BZ/NZ 58,561;
NBZ 72,961; U0 73,561. Every FIT row is evaluated through padded and pruned
heads at batch size one; maximum absolute raw-logit difference must be at most
`1e-5`. This proves inference transfer only. Only pruned logits may enter CAL,
DEV, or a later deployment.

## Calibration

After the scorer is frozen, each cell/arm predicts its matched TRAIN-CAL cohort
at deployment batch size one, then fits a deterministic positive per-action
affine map in CPU float64. Frozen settings are L2 `1e-6`,
minimum scale `1e-4`, minimum 20 observations and two examples of each class per
action, maximum 100 iterations, tolerance `1e-10`, and 10 ECE bins. All arms are
fitted and reported. For each path, acceptance is mandatory for that path's nine
candidate cells; failure of the other path's calibrators does not invalidate an
otherwise complete path. Control calibrators are descriptive and may remain
identity/unaccepted.

This is an explicit protocol change from V2.1i bias-only calibration. If a path
is nominated, its later production runner must reproduce positive per-action
affine calibration and requalify cross-action decisions. Raw logits are used
directly, without sigmoid, for raw ranking and causal comparisons. Calibrated
logits are used directly for calibrated ranking. Probabilities are used only for
proper-score, ECE, bias, and related deployability gates.

## Per-replica gates

Raw ranking is mandatory in every one of nine replicas. The NZ path requires Z,
BZ, NZ, and U0. The NBZ path requires Z, BZ, NZ, NBZ, and U0. Each required arm
must have aggregate ROC-AUC at least 0.65, gain of at least 0.10 over that
cohort's TRAIN-FIT-fitted all-action per-action-mean prior on DEV, and every
action AUC at least 0.60. N need not pass, and a non-required NBZ failure cannot
block the smaller NZ path.

For NZ and NBZ separately, every replica additionally requires:

- accepted finite calibration with positive scales and strict CAL BCE gain;
- calibrated DEV BCE no worse than raw DEV BCE;
- all-action BCE at most 0.98 and Brier at most 0.95 times its FIT prior;
- aggregate ECE at most 0.05;
- every action ECE at most 0.075, absolute bias at most 0.05, PR-AUC at least
  prevalence plus 0.05, and Brier no worse than its per-action FIT prior;
- calibrated aggregate/per-action ranking gates unchanged;
- FIT and DEV factual populations contain both classes for every action;
- factual BCE and Brier strictly beat the TRAIN-FIT factual per-action prior;
- one-sided candidate-family-alpha 0.025 episode-cluster lower bounds above zero
  for all-action and factual BCE and Brier improvements;
- all reported numbers finite.

The causal shuffle uses one donor episode for each recipient episode and maps
each root to the same ordinal root in that different episode. The entire
five-action score/probability row moves together. Twenty fixed derangements use
seed 71042, with one shared episode map and one explicitly expanded root map for
raw and calibrated tables. Median calibrated
shuffled-BCE/real-BCE must be at least 1.05, median raw aggregate AUC loss at
least 0.05, and every raw per-action AUC loss at least 0.03.

## Fixed inference and decision

Inference is conditional on the fixed 3x3 cohort/initialization grid; it does
not claim population-level robustness over training data or initialization.
For each arm/cell, compute full DEV AUC and all 384 leave-one-episode AUCs.
Comparison pseudovalues are `384*delta_full - 383*delta_delete`. Average the
nine fixed-cell pseudovalues per episode. A shared create-once int32
`50000 x 384` episode-index matrix from seed 62042 produces centered draws:
`grand_full + mean(sampled_pseudovalue) - mean(pseudovalue)`. Any degenerate
delete-one AUC fails closed. Quantiles use NumPy `linear`.
The canonical bootstrap matrix is int32 with SHA-256
`2f08ef405e90095ed4360ff30d57b22f5b4bdbcfd3f208f7e8bc07199e1915cc`;
the canonical `20 x 384` episode derangement matrix is int32 with SHA-256
`f69f0514910c88ac25cfd548466e084e23f32760fe3bcc2ee7eab468c8e9ce1f`;
its expanded `20 x 4608` root map is int32 with SHA-256
`9d6134737d2ac8d20f4463223eb7f2b2e485d2dd9a9854a4612128c791e5e95e`.

Familywise alpha 0.05 is Bonferroni-split across two candidate paths, so each
path uses a one-sided 0.025 lower quantile. Limbs within a path are a conjunctive
intersection-union test and receive no further split. Every one of nine point
deltas must also lie on the correct side.
Result fields report a generic one-sided lower bound, alpha, and confidence
level; candidate-path alpha 0.025 therefore has confidence level 0.975.

- NZ path: `NZ-N > 0` and `NZ-U0 > -0.025`.
- NBZ path: `NBZ-N > 0`, `NBZ-U0 > -0.025`, `NBZ-NZ > 0`, and
  `NBZ-BZ > -0.025`.
- Mechanistic confirmation only: `BZ-Z > 0` at one-sided 0.05. This limb cannot
  nominate an architecture.

Eligibility also requires all path-specific raw and calibrated replica gates.
Fixed minimality selection is NZ first, otherwise NBZ, otherwise none. Observed
AUC never breaks a tie and no alternate arm can be selected. Passing nominates
the entire masked-superset-train/pruned-deployment recipe for a later fresh,
from-scratch model-seed runner. It remains a diagnostic: qualification is
