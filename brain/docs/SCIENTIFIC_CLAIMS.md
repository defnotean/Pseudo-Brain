# Pseudo-Brain: Scientific Claims Ledger

Every major architectural, mechanistic, and empirical claim across the Pseudo-Brain repository is classified under strict epistemic discipline:
- `[MEASURED]`: Directly demonstrated and replicated through empirical benchmark evidence.
- `[INFERRED]`: Reasonable theoretical or mechanistic interpretation derived from measured data.
- `[HYPOTHESIS]`: Mechanistic proposal that is logically coherent but remains to be experimentally isolated.
- `[ASPIRATIONAL]`: Long-term engineering target or capability objective.
